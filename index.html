careful
Never run a "GTA VI PC port" exe from a post, a video or a Discord. That is where the malware is. VIctory ships no game files and is not playable yet.
VIctory: github.com/Marvisak/VIctory (GPL-3.0). This page: github.com/Cranefomo/gta6-pc-check.
